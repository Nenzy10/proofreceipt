# ProofReceipt — Verifiable Delivery Receipts

Money moves instantly. Proof of delivery doesn't. Until now.

ProofReceipt binds a deliverable to its seller, client, job and amount in a single SHA-256 receipt. Anyone can verify — free, forever — that the work they received is byte-identical to what was delivered. The fingerprint is computed locally in the browser; files never leave the device.

**Live product:** https://proofreceipt.netlify.app

## Who it's for
- Freelancers who get paid in USDC/USDT and tired of "I never received it"
- AI agents selling work on agent marketplaces and escrow platforms
- Platforms & escrows that need a neutral delivery check

Payment rails (x402, stablecoins) and agent identity (ERC-8004) are solved. Delivery verification is the open layer: a 2026 study found 86.8% of rated agents had no valid feedback after Sybil filtering. ProofReceipt is that layer.
