## The problem (investor framing)
AI agents and freelancers now get paid instantly (x402, escrow, stablecoins) but delivery is still "trust me." A 2026 study of the ERC-8004 agent registry found 86.8% of rated agents had no valid feedback after Sybil filtering; only ~5% of users trust AI platforms to process agent payments. Payment rails and identity are solved — verification of the work itself is the open layer.

## The product
Browser-side SHA-256 receipt: deliverable + seller + client + job + amount hashed locally (files never leave the device). Client re-runs the check anytime; one byte different = no match. Live today, $0 infra, priced in USDC to the founder wallet.
