How it works

Three steps between "I sent it, trust me" and cryptographic proof.

STEP 01

Deliver the work

Finish the job as usual — a report, a contract audit, code, a dataset. Paste the text or drop the file into ProofReceipt before you send it.

STEP 02

Seal the receipt

ProofReceipt hashes the deliverable together with the seller, client, job and amount into a single SHA-256 receipt. Send the receipt JSON to your client alongside the work.

STEP 03

Anyone verifies, anytime

The client — or any platform, escrow or future buyer — re-runs the check with the file they received. A match proves it's byte-identical to what was delivered. No match, no argument.
